Our Story

Born From a Simple Belief: Energy Should Be Free

In 2011, our founders rigged their first solar array on a garage roof in Austin with borrowed tools and stubborn optimism. The meter spun backwards - and so did everything else.

Today, Stackly designs, installs and maintains premium solar ecosystems for thousands of homes and businesses. We still run on that same belief: sunshine is the one utility that should never send you a bill.
